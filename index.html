Project Overview

Lucerne Grand — Development Overview

Lucerne Grand is CDL's most strategically positioned new launch in Singapore's western corridor. With Lakeside MRT at your doorstep, it occupies a 145,152 sq ft site at the gateway to the Jurong Lake District — Singapore's government-designated second CBD. 575 residential units across 5 x 16-storey blocks, with the Lucerne Galleria ground-floor retail podium.

Developer

City Developments Limited (CDL)

CDL is one of Singapore's most established property developers with over 60 years of experience. Listed on SGX, CDL's portfolio spans 145+ locations in 28 countries. For Lucerne Grand at Lakeside Drive, CDL committed $608M — a top bid 10.4% above the second bidder — signalling strong conviction in the JLD corridor.

CDL's track record of delivering quality homes on time, across projects like The Myst, Piccadilly Grand and Boulevard 88, provides buyers the assurance needed when committing capital before TOP.

MRT Connectivity

Lakeside MRT at Your Doorstep

Lucerne Grand's defining connectivity advantage is having Lakeside MRT at your doorstep. Jurong East interchange (NSL) is one stop away. The CBD is 25 minutes direct on the EWL. The Jurong Region Line adds further connectivity from 2027.
