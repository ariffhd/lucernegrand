Common Questions

Lucerne Grand FAQ

Answers to the most common questions about Lucerne Grand's facilities, construction and unit features. For anything not covered here, our sales team can help at the showflat.

Facilities & Amenities

Facilities Questions

How many EV charging lots are provided?
14 EV charging lots are provided.
Are the BBQ facilities gas or electric?
Electric BBQ facilities.
How many bicycle parking lots are provided, and where?
144 bicycle lots. Locations are shown on the official site plan.
What are the capacities of the clubhouse and facility rooms?
Lucerne Clubhouse I: 20 pax. Lucerne Clubhouse II: 18 pax. Gymnasium: 12 pax. Co-Working Lounge: 9 pax. Social Lounge: 8 pax.
What is the lap pool depth?
1,200mm. Width and swimming lane equivalence are to be confirmed.
How many side gates are there in total?
3 side gates in total, accessible by residents of all blocks.
Is there a provision for concierge service?
No, concierge service is not provided.
Are lift lobbies naturally ventilated or air-conditioned?
Basement lift lobbies are air-conditioned. Level 1 and above are naturally ventilated.
Are there ground-floor units with direct facility access?
Yes, selected ground-floor units have direct facility access — refer to the brochure and floor plans for specific stacks.
Construction & Building

Construction Questions

Is the building constructed using PPVC or conventional methods?
PPVC (Prefabricated Prefinished Volumetric Construction).
Is there a pneumatic waste disposal system?
Yes, Lucerne Grand has a pneumatic waste conveyance system.
How many lift shafts are there per block?
2 lift shafts per block.
When is the foundation expected to be laid?
Q3 2027, subject to change.
Unit Features

Unit Questions

Are ceiling fans installed? Can buyers opt out?
Ceiling fans are installed as standard and cannot be opted out of.
Is there a provision to install a bidet?
Yes, a provision is available at the master bathroom.
Is the 2.9m ceiling height at the living and dining area the same for all 4-Bedroom types?
Yes, the 2.9m ceiling height applies across all 4-Bedroom Premium unit types.
Can buyers opt to remove the study or entertainment room wall for the 4-Bedroom Premium + Study / + Entertainment types?
Yes, there is an option to remove the wall. The cut-off date for this option is to be confirmed — check with our sales team.
Maintenance & MCST

Maintenance Questions

Does rental income from the commercial units (Lucerne Galleria) go to the MCST?
No, commercial rental income does not go back to the residential MCST.
Does the residential maintenance fee include a portion for the commercial shops?
No, residential maintenance fees are separate from commercial upkeep.
Answers reflect information available as of the brochure release and are subject to change without prior notice. Some details (marked TBC in official materials) are still being finalised by the developer — please confirm the latest specifications with our sales team or the Sale and Purchase Agreement.
