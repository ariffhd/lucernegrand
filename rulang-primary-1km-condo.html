How P1 Registration Works

Why 1km Proximity Matters

Singapore's Primary 1 Registration Exercise uses a phased system. 1km status in Phase 2C(S) gives priority over those in the 1-2km band and the open ballot Phase 2C. For families targeting Rulang Primary, Lucerne Grand's 1km qualification removes the uncertainty of the open ballot.

Always verify the current 1km boundary with MOE's School Finder at moe.gov.sg before purchase, as boundaries may be revised.
